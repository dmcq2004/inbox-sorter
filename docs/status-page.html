How the calendar sync works

When07:00 and 17:30 daily. If the laptop was asleep or off, it runs as soon as it's back on while you're logged in.
ReadsNew Trinity and Young Explorers emails since the last run, new newsletters (read by script, no browser) and their attachments, which a Google Apps Script copies into Drive every 15 minutes.
WritesCreates or updates school events only, in the same style as the rest of your calendar. Optional events (coffee mornings, PAFT and community events) are included, marked "(optional)" and free. At most 12 changes per run.
Leaves outYoung Explorers bookings, which the family decides, and anything only for other year groups.
NeverSends, trashes or deletes anything, or touches non-school events. Instructions inside emails are ignored.
ModelClaude Opus 5.5, pinned. Sonnet 5.5 was tested on the same emails: it was 3× faster but skimmed the emails and reported things it hadn't checked.
UnsureAnything unclear goes under "Needs Dom" in the run's report (logs/calendar-sync/) instead of the calendar.
UndoEvery change is logged with its event id in logs/runs/.

Decisions made

  • Reuse and tidy the existing labelsKeep the Triage/* scheme and your sender folders, and prune the empty ones.
  • Claude does all the sortingNo Gmail filters for you to set up, since you read mail in your phone's Mail app.
  • Archive only low-value mailThe 19.5k backlog is a separate job for later.
  • The school module covers Trinity and Young ExplorersBright Horizons emails are app notifications, so they're labelled Nursery and archived.
  • This project has replaced the old daily school jobThe claude.ai school-comms-triage job (email → Notion → calendar) was switched off on 7 Nov. Its existing events are reused, never duplicated.
  • The sync runs on Opus 5.5Chosen over Sonnet after a side-by-side test: the job runs unattended, so thoroughness matters more than speed.
  • School events keep your existing calendar styleClosures are green all-day "(Seb)" events, events are "Trinity: …", and early pick-ups are timed events that block your diary.
  • The school calendar is hands-offThe scheduled sync applies changes itself, within strict limits, and flags anything unclear for you. Everything is logged and can be undone.
  • Optional events go in; club bookings don'tThings you could go to are added as free "(optional)" events. Young Explorers sessions are left to you.
  • Keep attachment copies until the summer term endsThey're small, and they let Claude re-read letters later. Purge the year's files after 20 Jul 2027, since you're on free Google storage.
  • It runs on the laptop for now, then the Raspberry PiSame rules and scripts on both; the Pi gets them from GitHub.

How mail gets labelled

Each sorted thread gets one priority label and, optionally, one folder label. FYI and Bulk are archived, so they leave the Inbox but stay in their folder.

Triage/ActionNeeds doing within a week: pay, sign, reply, book. Stays in the inbox.
Triage/NeedsDomA real person or an important account, with no hard deadline. Stays in the inbox.
Triage/FYIDeliveries, statements, notifications. Archived.
Triage/BulkMarketing, newsletters, surveys. Archived.
School/TrinityNew label. Trinity C of E School Lewisham.
School/Young ExplorersNew label. Breakfast, after-school and holiday club.
NewslettersNew label. Latent Space, AI Secret and similar.

Things to know

Some staff signatures link to a spam site

The school's real website is trinitylewisham.org.uk (it redirects to trinity.lewisham.sch.uk). Some staff email signatures link to trinitylewisham.org instead, without the .uk, and that address serves gambling spam. Don't tap links in signatures.

Nothing new goes into the Notion school list

The old job that filled the Notion School & Childcare list is switched off. Outstanding actions come back with the action tracker and shared dashboard (roadmap item 2): built to avoid duplicates, and visible to Rosie.

The sync needs the laptop on

Runs only happen while the laptop is on and you're logged in; a missed run catches up when it wakes. Moving to the Pi removes this.

Safety rules: Claude never sends, forwards, trashes or marks spam, and never deletes calendar events. Gmail commands are dry runs unless you say "live". The school calendar sync changes school events only, and logs every change.
