How the scheduled runs work

Cron on the Raspberry Pi runs at 07:00 and 17:30: first it fetches any rule changes from GitHub, then runs the school calendar sync, then the inbox sort. Reports and the undo log stay on the Pi, and Claude reads them from the laptop.

The sort only adds or removes labels: it can't create or delete labels, send, or trash anything. When it isn't sure about an email, it marks it NeedsDom and leaves it in your inbox. It sorts at most 100 emails per run. The calendar sync works like this:

When07:00 and 17:30 daily, just before the inbox sort. Runs on the Pi, which is always on. If a run is missed, the next one picks up everything since the last run that worked.
ReadsNew Trinity and Young Explorers emails since the last run, new newsletters (read by script, no browser) and their attachments, which a Google Apps Script copies into Drive every 15 minutes.
FilesLabels the school emails it reads (since 19 Nov). Anything unresolved, such as an attachment it couldn't read, stays in your inbox as NeedsDom.
WritesCreates or updates school events only, in the same style as the rest of your calendar. Optional events (coffee mornings, PAFT and community events) are included, marked "(optional)" and free. At most 12 changes per run.
Leaves outYoung Explorers bookings, which the family decides, and anything only for other year groups.
NeverSends, trashes or deletes anything, or touches non-school events. Instructions inside emails are ignored.
ModelClaude Opus 5.5, pinned. Sonnet 5.5 was tested on the same emails: it was 3× faster but skimmed the emails and reported things it hadn't checked.
UnsureAnything unclear goes under "Needs Dom" in the run's report (logs/calendar-sync/) instead of the calendar.
UndoEvery change is logged with its event id in logs/runs/.

How mail gets labelled

*Triage/NeedsDomA real person or an important account, with no hard deadline. Stays in the inbox.
*Triage/FYIDeliveries, statements, notifications. Archived.

Things to know

Some staff signatures link to a spam site

The school's real website is trinitylewisham.org.uk (it redirects to trinity.lewisham.sch.uk). Some staff email signatures link to trinitylewisham.org instead, without the .uk, and that address serves gambling spam. Don't tap links in signatures.

Nothing new goes into the Notion school list

The old job that filled the Notion School & Childcare list is switched off. Outstanding actions come back with the action tracker and shared dashboard (roadmap item 2): built to avoid duplicates, and visible to Rosie.

The Pi's Claude sign-in can expire

It had expired after a month unused, and the runs keep it fresh now. Sign-ins end on a fixed date, so expect the Pi's to end about a month after 23 Nov. When it does, the runs and the "Inbox (Pi)" session on your phone both stop. To renew, run claude in ~/Emails at the Pi and type /login. The Pi needs to stay on and connected to Wi-Fi.

The school warned about a phishing email

On 17 Nov Trinity said an email from "Rachel Samuels" with Kady Tait's signature, asking you to open a link, is phishing. Don't open it.

Safety rules: Claude sends, replies or forwards only when you say so in /inbox, and shows you each email first. It never trashes or marks spam, and never deletes calendar events. Gmail commands are dry runs unless you say "live". The school calendar sync changes school events only, and logs every change.
